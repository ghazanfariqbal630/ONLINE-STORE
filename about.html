About Us

Who we are

Asaan Digital Bazaar is an online store that makes shopping simple for everyone in Pakistan. We bring together handmade crafts, jewelry, clothing, gemstones and organic food from trusted local artisans and sellers — all in one place.

Our promise

  • Authentic products — every item is checked before it is sent to you.
  • Cash on Delivery — pay only when your parcel arrives.
  • Delivery all over Pakistan — usually within 3–5 working days.
  • 7 days easy return — if the item is not as described, we will make it right.

Supporting local artisans

Many of our products are made by hand by skilled craftspeople. When you buy from us, you help small businesses and local artisans grow.
