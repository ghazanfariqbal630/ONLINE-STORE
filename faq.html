Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, open the cart, fill in your name, phone and address, then press Place order via WhatsApp. Your order is sent to us on WhatsApp and we call you to confirm.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?

Yes. Cash on Delivery is available all over Pakistan — you pay when the parcel reaches you.

What other payment methods do you accept?

You can also pay by bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. We will share the account details after you place your order.

How much is delivery and how long does it take?

Delivery is Rs. 250, and free on orders over Rs. 5,000. Orders usually arrive in 3–5 working days.

Which cities do you deliver to?

We deliver to all major cities and towns across Pakistan.

What is your return policy?

If the item is damaged or not as described, contact us within 7 days of delivery and we will replace it or refund you. Items must be unused and in original packaging.

How can I track my order?

Message us on WhatsApp with your name or phone number and we will share your order status and tracking details.

Are your products authentic?

Yes. Every product is checked before dispatch. If you ever receive something that is not as described, our return policy covers you.
